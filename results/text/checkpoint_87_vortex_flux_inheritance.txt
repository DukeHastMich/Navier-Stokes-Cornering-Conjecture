Checkpoint 87 - instantaneous vortex-tube flux inheritance
Kinematic divergence-free example; NOT a Navier-Stokes time evolution.

For any instantaneous vortex tube whose side surface is tangent to omega, div omega=0 implies equal vorticity flux through every cross-section.
The toy field uses an axisymmetric flux function psi(r,z)=Gamma/(2pi)[1-exp(-(r/a(z))^2)].
It narrows/widens with z while maintaining flux Gamma=1 across every transverse section.

Interpretation for the renormalization argument:
  Merely choosing a smaller geometric ruler inside the SAME coherent instantaneous vortex tube does not manufacture a larger circulation Reynolds number Gamma/nu. The tube strength is the same across its sections.
  Therefore a same-tube scale escape can reset the geometry coefficient A at a smaller ruler, but it does not by itself increase Re_Gamma.
  To make Re_Gamma grow from generation to generation, the dynamics must change the tube strength in time, aggregate flux from other structures, or cease to admit a coherent-tube description.
