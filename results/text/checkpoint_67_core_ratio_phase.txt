Checkpoint 67 - finite-core ratio phase table
Model: R^2dot=-2 c_g Gamma; bdot=-c_s Gamma b/R^2 + 4 nu; y=b/R^2.
tau=-ln(R^2/R0^2): dy/dtau=2/(c_g Re_Gamma)+(1-lambda)y, lambda=c_s/(2c_g).
For lambda>1: y*=2/[c_g Re_Gamma (lambda-1)], so a/R -> sqrt(y*) > 0 at finite Re.
For lambda<=1: y does not approach zero; it grows (linearly at lambda=1, exponentially for lambda<1).
Illustrative normalization c_g=0.5 (2 c_g=1).
